I’m a fourth year PhD student at Tsinghua University majoring in computer science. I’m a member of the THU-KEG laboratory under the supervise of Prof. Juanzi Li.

Generally, I’m interested in the research of large language models (LLMs). My goal is to build trustworthy AI systems. Now I’m working on several research topics, including:

  • Trustworthy evaluation for LLM: Shortcut Neuron@ACL25, Knowledge-to-Jailbreak@KDD25
  • Cognitive evaluation for LLM: KoLA@ICLR24, R-Eval@KDD24, LongBench-v2@ACL25,
  • LLM application for education: LittleMu@CIKM23, LongWriter-V@ACMMM25

Beyond research, I have also been an intern at the post-training team of Zhipu since July, 2024. I'm happy to cooperate with my teammates on building large reasoning models like GLM-4.5 and GLM-4.5V.
